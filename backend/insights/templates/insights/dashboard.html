5. Data and method

Cleaning rules

  1. Remove exact duplicate rows.
  2. Set cancellations aside (used for the return rate).
  3. Remove sales fully reversed by a later cancellation (same customer, product and quantity).
  4. Keep real products only (drop postage, fees, adjustments, vouchers).
  5. Keep positive quantities and prices.

Limitations

  • One retailer, mostly business customers; results describe this business, not retail in general.
  • {% widthratio results.funnel.rows_with_customer_id results.funnel.clean_rows 100 %}% of sales lines have a customer ID; customer analyses use those only.
  • Basket rules and segments are associations, not causal effects.
